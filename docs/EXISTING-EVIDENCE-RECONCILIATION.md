## Operational completion and scholarly limits

Every existing reading assertion and declared identity row has a source-qualified disposition. Literal source files are hash-frozen for this reconciliation-only checkpoint. Validators reject changes in coverage, removed apparatus, candidate promotion, unregistered catalogue namespaces, conflicting identifiers and fabricated physical or expert certification. Use `research/reference-reconciliation-v2.json` and `research/identity-reconciliation-v2.json` alongside the original sources and `research/source-reconciliation.json`.

Deterministic accounting is complete for these declared views. It does not settle conflicting materials, object equivalence or uncertain ancient signs. Those questions retain exact locators and documented evidence/review dependencies rather than being marked resolved. Public viewing of CHIC pages is used for limited attributed facts; no source facsimiles, photographs or PDFs are redistributed.
